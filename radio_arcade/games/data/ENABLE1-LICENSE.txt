ENABLE word list

The ENABLE master word list, WORD.LST, was compiled by Mendel Cooper and
Alan Beale with contributions from the Scrabble community. This project
distributes the lowercase ENABLE1 word list from:

https://github.com/lexieboren/Scrabble/blob/master/enable1.txt

The ENABLE list was formally released into the public domain. The original
notice asks game designers to credit the list's originators. ENABLE is an
alternative word list and is not an official tournament word authority.

The word list is data, separate from the Radio Arcade source code. Its
SHA-256 digest is
3f16130220645692ed49c7134e24a18504c2ca55b3c012f7290e3e77c63b1a89.
